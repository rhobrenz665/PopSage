# PopSage infrastructure options — personal training edition

Status: **Design interview complete through Q58. The founder confirmed shared understanding of the free-tier training architecture and product boundaries. Engineering validation remains; no implementation or provisioning is authorized.** Public official sources checked **3 November 2026**. This revision supersedes the earlier paid/pilot recommendation.

The project is personal software-engineering training using fictional businesses, sample sites, and approved test documents. Initial total service budget is **US$0/month**. A possible **US$50/month future scenario is comparison only**, not permission to activate billing or purchase anything. No real customers, commercial pilot, or country-specific training residency requirement is assumed. Reassess hosting eligibility and data obligations before real use.

## Proposed zero-cost route

Use **Vercel Hobby + Neon Free + Cloudflare Workers Free + Trigger.dev Free**. Q17–Q19 approve Vercel for Next.js, Neon for database/auth/private originals, and Trigger.dev for durable jobs; Q15 approves PartyServer for realtime; Q16 approves Workers AI for initial classification, generation, and embeddings with OpenAI readiness from the outset. Exact models, regions, onboarding flow, operational settings, and account eligibility still require validation.

The initial embedding-provider preference is explicitly revised by Q16: Cloudflare is active initially; OpenAI must have a separate future adapter. Q15 approves PartyServer/PartySocket on Cloudflare as the realtime successor. Deterministic test doubles can validate contracts without charges but must never be represented as genuine model/retrieval quality validation.

### PartyKit retirement — independently rechecked

The official migration notice says hosted PartyKit and `partykit deploy` ceased on **23 October 2026**, including CLI deployment into a user's own Cloudflare account. Existing self-owned Workers keep running. It directs new projects to **PartyServer + Wrangler**, preserving the rooms/connection model and PartySocket compatibility. Treat the older marketing homepage's free-hosting description as stale relative to the dated notice. ## AI choices: recurring free versus credits versus paid

**Approved provider and evaluation candidates:** Q30 selects `@cf/meta/llama-3.1-8b-instruct-fp8` for initial classification/generation evaluation and `@cf/baai/bge-small-en-v1.5` for English embedding evaluation. The latter produces 384 dimensions with a 512-token input maximum; chunk accordingly. They remain replaceable configuration and cannot be activated until shared quality, retrieval, latency, and free-quota gates pass. Selection is not evidence that they meet the PRD targets. [Text model](https://developers.cloudflare.com/workers-ai/models/llama-3.1-8b-instruct-fp8/), [Embedding model](https://developers.cloudflare.com/workers-ai/models/bge-small-en-v1.5/).

**Future OpenAI adapter and activation constraint:** direct `text-embedding-3-small` is priced at $0.02/M tokens; no recurring zero-price direct API entitlement was established. A provider's rate-limit row named “Free” is not proof that inference is free. Trial, promotional, or existing account credits cannot be assumed. Design a separate OpenAI adapter for classification, generation, and embeddings from the beginning, but do not invoke billable APIs at the initial $0 budget. [Official OpenAI model pricing](https://developers.openai.com/api/docs/models/text-embedding-3-small).

**Optional future OpenAI credit investigation, not the selected baseline:** Vercel AI Gateway advertises $5 recurring monthly free credit, but current documentation limits it to eligible models with lower rate limits and no free-tier BYOK. Its catalog lists OpenAI embeddings, yet the fetched page did not establish this account's free-credit eligibility. Verify eligibility and credit exhaustion behavior before relying on it. Do not assume the credit applies to every model or buy credits to make a model available. [Current gateway terms](https://vercel.com/docs/ai-gateway/pricing), [Embedding listing](https://vercel.com/ai-gateway/models/text-embedding-3-small), [Eligible-model catalog](https://vercel.com/ai-gateway/models?freeTier=true).

**Alternative free provider:** Gemini offers free input/output on selected models with model/account-specific quotas; its free tier uses content to improve products. It adds another provider and is not the default proposal. Only fictional approved data should be considered, and no billing upgrade is authorized. [Gemini pricing and data distinction](https://aistudio.google.com/docs/pricing).

A hosted free AI route exists today, but no provider promises perpetual unchanged allowances or production-level availability at $0. When unavailable, stop AI work and expose a clear unavailable/quota state while preserving human chat. Do not rotate accounts, silently fall back to paid models, or lower grounding requirements to get an answer.

## Small training usage calculations

These are illustrative capacities, not traffic forecasts or a new performance requirement:

- **Database:** 0.25 CU × 160 awake hours = 40 CU-hours, under the 100-hour allowance; 0.25 CU × 730 always-awake hours = 182.5, over it. Polling can prevent useful sleep. Storage/index growth must also fit.
- **Documents:** 50 originals averaging 5 MB use about 250 MB of object storage. This does not include database chunks/vectors or branches. Full three-workspace maximums of 100 × 20 MB each would be roughly 6 GB, exceeding 5 GB free originals storage. Product limits are not a promise that every free deployment can fill them simultaneously.
- **Jobs:** 1,000 runs × (30 seconds × $0.0000338 Small-1x/second + $0.000025/run) = **$1.039 of metered usage**, covered by the recurring $5 allowance if other work stays within it. Retries, other tasks, and larger machines consume more. This is not a charge authorization. [Execution rates](https://trigger.dev/pricing).
- **AI:** using the published neuron rates, 100 messages/day at 4,000 combined classifier/answer input and 500 output tokens each consume about `0.4 × 13,778 + 0.05 × 26,128 = 6,818` neurons. Another 100k embedding tokens consume about 184 neurons: approximately **7,002/day**, below 10,000. Extra validation calls/retries count. At 200 otherwise identical messages, generation alone exceeds the allowance. [Neuron rates](https://developers.cloudflare.com/workers-ai/platform/pricing/).

A separate load test should use controlled fixtures and a cost-free stub for expensive calls, followed by a small real-provider run. Stub latency/quality does not establish real AI performance. Neither the earlier peak target nor prior commercial pilot assumptions establish free-tier capacity.

## Data destinations and regions

US East is the approved training preference where supported because Vercel functions default to `iad1`, Neon storage is offered in N. Virginia, and Trigger.dev documents US East workers. Confirm the actual selectable region for every account/service before setup. Co-location reduces hops; it does not establish end-to-end residency, constrain Cloudflare processing to one location, or remove the requirement to document data destinations. [Vercel regions](https://vercel.com/docs/functions/configuring-functions/region), [Neon storage regions](https://neon.com/docs/storage/overview), [Trigger.dev regions](https://trigger.dev/changelog/multi-region-workers).

Data flows should be explicit before implementation:

- Vercel handles dashboard/widget HTTP, app requests, and potentially operational logs. Static assets use its CDN.
- Neon stores users/sessions, tenant records, transcripts, chunks/vectors, and private originals in the configured project. Auth is colocated with the database. [Managed auth](https://neon.com/docs/auth/about).
- Trigger.dev workers receive job identifiers, fetch document content for parsing, and may store payloads/logs; prefer identifiers over document text in job payloads. Worker region is not proof that all service metadata remains there.
- Cloudflare handles realtime connections and selected AI inputs/outputs. DO location hints are not strict residency guarantees. Workers AI states no model training and limited retention for standard inference, with exceptions for selected stateful/logging features. Do not infer that database region pins AI processing. [DO location](https://developers.cloudflare.com/durable-objects/reference/data-location/), [Workers AI data policy](https://developers.cloudflare.com/workers-ai/platform/data-usage/).

Re-evaluate destinations, logs, retention/deletion, acceptable providers, and hosting eligibility before accepting real documents or customer data. Keep auth emails/accounts and secrets out of fictional fixtures where possible; actual login records still deserve normal protection.

## Keep the design small and replaceable

Approved AI boundaries and proposed infrastructure boundaries; design only, not implementation work:

- One Next.js app, one shared Neon project with enforced workspace scope, one realtime service, one job service. Avoid another cache, vector database, or function runtime until a measured need exists.
- Keep provider credentials and URLs in server-only environment configuration. Suggested configuration categories: database URL, auth endpoint, object-store endpoint/bucket, realtime host, job credentials, independent generation provider/model, classification provider/model, embedding provider/model/dimensions/index version, and usage limits. Never put service secrets in public widget configuration.
- Use separate provider-independent interfaces for generation, classification, and embeddings, plus narrow storage/realtime/job boundaries. Design both Cloudflare Workers AI and OpenAI adapters for all AI capabilities now; only Cloudflare is initially active. This explicit dual-adapter requirement supersedes the earlier single-adapter recommendation. Avoid a universal plugin framework or additional unrequested providers.
- Persist model/embedding configuration with indexed content. Swapping embeddings requires re-embedding into a separate compatible index/version before cutover; changing environment variables alone cannot make old vectors compatible.
- Preserve authorization, private storage, idempotent processing, retry bounds, complete-answer validation, ownership races, source-removal checks, and transcript recovery. Free limits affect availability/capacity, not these correctness requirements.
- Persist durable changes and outbox events atomically in Neon Postgres. Attempt immediate PartyServer publication after commit and retain a retrying dispatcher. Use stable IDs and transactional monotonic stream sequences; consumers deduplicate and recover gaps. Quarantine poison events without dropping/overtaking. Apply Postgres RLS alongside application authorization, keep accepted messages immutable, and keep full message bodies out of workspace inbox rooms. These controls remain required on free tiers.
- Keep a visible quota state and conservative application caps. Q32 sets an initial 80% estimated daily Cloudflare allowance admission stop and two concurrent AI pipelines, both configurable after measurement. Do not assume alert emails are spending caps. At $0, remain on free plans, decline paid-only features, and stop at quota exhaustion. Use provider subdomains rather than buying a domain.

## Q16 architecture implications — approved planning requirements

[ADR 0005](../adr/0005-independent-ai-providers.md) defines independent capability contracts, adapters, configuration validation, and an embedding migration strategy. [PRD 03](../prds/03-ai-support-and-handoff.md#provider-independent-evaluation-plan) defines shared quality, classification, retrieval, switching, and cost-safety evaluation.

- Classification, generation, and embeddings each select their own provider/model. Mixed configurations require no business-logic changes; they remain subject to activation, model-capability, quality, and cost checks.
- Normalize provider-specific payloads and operational errors at the adapters. Keep tenant scope, evidence selection, source attribution, grounding validation, and handoff outside those adapters. Provider/model and configuration versions accompany evaluation records.
- Inactive OpenAI adapters require no key and make no calls. Selecting OpenAI or providing a key is not paid-use approval. Do not enable billing, automatic paid fallback, or live paid tests. Cloudflare quota exhaustion must preserve human-chat fallback.
- Embedding profile changes require a separate compatible index/version, explicit re-embedding, completeness and relevance checks, atomic query/index cutover, and bounded rollback. Match provider/model/revision, dimensions, preprocessing, and distance configuration; equal dimensions are not sufficient. Account for extra storage and reindex quota before migration; pause if the free tier cannot accommodate it.
- Test every capability selection combination using cost-free transports/fixtures, then evaluate active Cloudflare models against the common labeled dataset within quotas when implementation is authorized. OpenAI live quality remains unverified until separately authorized and executed. A proposed adapter design is not a working implementation.

The recurring service-cost target remains US$0. Designing the second adapter adds development work but does not authorize a paid provider account or live API usage. Current model prices, quotas, and data policies above retain their original research verification date; this edit records architecture approval, not a fresh pricing check.
