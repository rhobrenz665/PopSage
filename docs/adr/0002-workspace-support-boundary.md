# Use the workspace as the business support boundary

Accepted on 3 November 2026 during the founder’s design interview. One workspace represents one support operation with a shared knowledge base and inbox, even when its widget is embedded on multiple websites. Brands needing distinct answers must use separate workspaces: this avoids introducing per-website knowledge routing and permissions into v1, at the cost of requiring separate setup for those brands.

Sharing a workspace does not promise that anonymous visitor identity or conversation history follows a person across its websites. Visitor continuity remains constrained by the supported browser/session design.
